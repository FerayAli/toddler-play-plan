Stay close Hold a hand on stairs and stay beside the couch for climbs. Balls, sock-balls, and toys must be too big to fit in the mouth. Skip marbles, coins, beads, and bottle caps. If they peel painter’s tape, the game is over for the day. Children learn these skills on different clocks; if walking or words feel far behind other children the same age, ask the pediatrician.

Household kit: cushions, laundry basket, painter’s tape, kitchen towel, socks, a soft ball, a cardboard box, a pot and wooden spoon, two cups, a picture book, a stuffed animal, three lidded containers.
